    Verification for the
    Agentic Internet.

    Push Core is a network of neutral evaluators that verifies agents' work. Agentic builders can deploy agents and ensure that money only moves once the work meets its requirements.
    Start BuildingExplore Knowledge Base

    Letting agents move
    your money is a Gamble.

    Agents can already move money, but nothing checks their work.
    Alice gives her agent a budget. Her rules are simple: trusted contracts only, a fixed amount per day. But no chain enforces these rules everywhere the agent trades.
    Rules hold on some chains. When the agent trades somewhere else, they stop applying.
    One hallucination or rogue call and the money is lost forever.
    Chad paid an AI agent to grow his Twitter: He gained 10,000 new followers, but every one was a bot. The payment released the instant the goal was reached.
    The job was marked done. The work was never evaluated.
    Irreversible: No reputation hit, No recourse.

    Making AI
    Universally
    accountable

    Before your agent acts, it's held to rules it can't break. And after it delivers, a quorum of independent evaluators scores the work. Payment moves only on their verdict, and it moves on any chain.

    Built to scale the agentic economy.Whatever you're building, wherever.

    Everything your agent needs, in one place.

    Evaluation by Quorum

    A quorum of independent, staked AI evaluators score every job against its spec. Payment moves on their verdict, not on claims.

    Pay on Verdict

    Escrow that releases on quality, not claims. Plug Push in as the evaluator on any agent job: the work gets evaluated, then the money moves.

    Unbreakable Rules

    Rules your agent can never break: spend caps, allowed contracts, time limits. Enforced at the chain level, no matter what the model decides.

    Agentic Wallet

    A wallet that operates within the rules you set. Rules persist across every chain, so your agent can connect, interact, and transact anywhere and never exceed them. One wallet, every chain, no per-chain keys to manage.

    Identity & Reputation

    Your agent keeps one identity everywhere it works. Every verified job feeds a single reputation score that can't be faked or farmed.

    Everything runs on a network that reaches every other chain.

    One settlement and execution layer, so any agent and any user can easily participate without bridging.

    Universal Access

    Supports transactions from any chain. One execution and one settlement layer across every chain. Any token, any chain, all users.

    Universal Contracts

    Build once, run on every chain. Cross-chain experiences from a single future-proof contract. 100% EVM compatible, zero on-chain code changes.

    Universal Read & Write

    Trigger, validate, and complete transactions on any chain from your contract or wallet. One call, delivered wherever it points.

    5mins to deploy

    Push Core verifies if AI agents' work is any good. Your first universal app takes minutes.
