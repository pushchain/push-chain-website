Meet [Zappi](http://zappi.to), our newest inductee in the Push Chain Universal App ecosystem\!

[Zappi](http://zappi.to) is an anychain to anychain payments app built for freelancers, creators, and business owners of all sizes to request, receive, and settle invoices onchain.

Zappi is powered by PUSD \- A universal stablecoin on Push Chain that unifies USDC and USDT liquidity across all major EVM, non EVM chains.

Here’s what it unpacks…

# Powered by PUSD and Push Chain

Under the hood, Zappi uses [PUSD](http://pusd.push.org) as its stablecoin layer.

**PUSD is a universal stablecoin on Push Chain that provides a common dollar layer on top of crypto unifying fragmented liquidity from USDC, USDT across all popular chains.**

Users can deposit supported stablecoins, mint PUSD 1:1, use it across Push Chain apps, and redeem into supported stablecoins.

This matters because payments need a common dollar layer.

Without PUSD, every payment app has to think through different versions of the same dollar: USDC on one chain, USDT on another, wrapped assets, bridge flows, liquidity routes, and withdrawal preferences.

Read [more about PUSD here](https://push.org/blog/introducing-pusd/)

# What exactly does Zappi do?

Global digital payments are now being taken over by Stablecoins.

These solutions are fast, secure, instant and incredibly cheaper than traditional SWIFT/ACH counterparts. And they run 24x7.

But stablecoins are still isolated within chain boundaries. USDC on Solana and USDC on Ethereum may represent the same dollar value, but they are not automatically interchangeable in practice.

And this is a huge bottleneck that very few teams are trying to solve.

*A user might have USDC on Base.*
*The recipient might prefer USDT on another chain.*
*The app might support only one network.*
*The payment might need a bridge, a swap, multiple approvals, extra gas, and a lot of waiting.*

That is too much friction for something as simple as “send $100.”

Stablecoin payments should feel simple: create a request, share a link, receive funds.

**Paid from any supported stablecoin, through any chain.**

That is what Zappi is designed for.
